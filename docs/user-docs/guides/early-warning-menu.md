# Early Warning Menu - User Guide

## What is Early Warning?

**Early Warning** is the place where the system collects overtime alerts: days when a section used too much of its daily overtime budget (zone **Kuning** or **Merah**). It has its own item in the sidebar, so you can reach it in one click. The list of alerts and the follow-up form are being added step by step; this guide covers how to find the menu and who can use it.

## Who Can Use It?

| Role                | What they see                       |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Administrator**   | Alerts of every department          |
| **Manager**         | Alerts of their own department only |
| **Team Leader**     | Alerts of their own department only |
| **Operator / User** | No access. The menu is not shown.   |

## How to Use

### Open Early Warning

1. Open the **Early Warning** menu in the sidebar, under **Operations & Overtime**, just below **Employee Reports**
2. The Early Warning page opens
3. The menu stays highlighted while you are on the Early Warning page and on any alert opened from it

### Read the red badge

1. Look at the **Early Warning** menu: a small red number means there are **open Merah alerts** this month
2. An alert is open until a follow-up for it has been **sent**. A saved draft still counts as open
3. When the sidebar is collapsed to icons only, the number is replaced by a small red dot
4. No badge means there is nothing open

> 💡 **Tip:** The number refreshes about once a minute, so it may take a moment to drop after you send a follow-up.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Why can't I see the Early Warning menu?**
A: The menu is only for Administrators, Managers and Team Leaders. Operators do not have it.

**Q: Why do I only see my own department?**
A: Managers and Team Leaders only work with their own department. Only Administrators see every department.

**Q: What happens if I open an alert from another department?**
A: The system shows an **Access Restricted** page. Go back to **Early Warning** and pick an alert of your department.

**Q: What happens if the date in the link is wrong?**
A: You are returned to the Early Warning page with a message that the date is invalid.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue                                        | Solution                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The menu is missing                          | Check your role in the profile badge. Ask an Administrator if your role should have access |
| Access Restricted page                       | The alert belongs to another department. Return to **Early Warning**                       |
| The badge still shows a number after sending | Wait about a minute and refresh the page                                                   |

# User Preferences & Display Standards - User Guide

## What is User Preferences & Display Standards?

User Preferences allows every user in the factory to customize their visual interface theme (Light Mode, Dark Mode, or System Default) and choose which operational notification alerts they want to receive. It also provides a clear overview of the fixed factory display standards (Western Indonesia Time / WIB, Indonesian date formatting, and Rupiah currency) enforced for all operational audits.

## How to Use

### Changing Your Theme Mode

1. Click your name or avatar at the bottom-left of the sidebar to open the user menu.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. In the settings navigation on the left, click **Preferences**.
4. In the **Theme Mode** section, click one of the three theme cards:
    - **Light Mode**: Clean bright interface recommended for daytime work and brightly lit office environments.
    - **Dark Mode**: High-contrast dark theme optimized for night shift supervisors and reduced eye strain.
    - **System Default**: Automatically matches your computer or mobile device's operating system appearance.
5. Notice that your screen immediately updates to the selected theme.
6. Click **Save Preferences** at the bottom to store your choice permanently.

> 💡 **Tip:** You do not need to refresh the page when changing themes. The interface applies your selected mode instantly.

### Configuring Notification Channels & Alerts

1. Open **Settings → Preferences**.
2. Scroll to the **Notification Channels & Alerts** section.
3. Check or uncheck the notification options according to your role:
    - **SPKL Document Pending Reminders**: Receive reminders when submitted overtime requests still have physical SPKL paper forms waiting to be uploaded before the grace period expires.
    - **Overtime Budget Threshold & Burn Alert**: Receive alerts when your department or section approaches warning (100%) or danger (115%) levels of their monthly overtime budget.
    - **Overtime Approval Status Notifications**: Receive instant updates when your submitted overtime submissions are verified, approved, or returned for revision.
4. Click **Save Preferences**.
5. A green confirmation toast will appear confirming your settings are saved.

### Viewing Company Display Standards

At the bottom of the Preferences page, the **Plant Standards & Global Formats** card shows the fixed formatting rules enforced across all manufacturing plants:

- **Timezone**: `WIB (Asia/Jakarta, UTC+07:00)` — All timesheets, clock-in records, and shift schedules use Western Indonesia Time.
- **Date Format**: `DD/MM/YYYY` (for example, `07/09/2026`).
- **Currency Format**: `Rupiah (IDR)` (for example, `Rp 25.000,00`).

> 💡 **Note:** These display formats are locked company-wide to guarantee that shift handovers, audit reports, and payroll calculations remain completely consistent across all departments.

# Authentication & Access Control - User Guide

## What is Authentication & Access Control?

The OT-CapEx system helps factory operators, team leaders, and plant managers log in securely, view their shift assignments, and manage overtime tasks according to their specific responsibilities.

Depending on whether you work on the factory floor or in the plant administration office, you can log in using either your **Employee Identification Number (NPK)** or your **corporate email address**. Once signed in, the system automatically tailors your workspace to match your assigned role and production line.

---

## How to Use

### Logging In to the System

1. Open the system login page in your web browser.
2. In the **Email or NPK** field, enter either:
    - Your numeric NPK badge number (for example: `EMP-10029`), OR
    - Your company email address (for example: `operator@factory.com`).
3. In the **Password** field, enter your confidential account password.
    - Click the **Show / Hide Eye icon** on the right of the password field if you wish to verify your input.
4. (Optional) Check the **Remember me** checkbox if you are using your assigned computer.
5. Click the **Log in to System** button.

> 💡 **Tip:** NPK entry is not case-sensitive. You can type `emp-10029` or `EMP-10029`.

---

### Reading Your Operational Dashboard

Once logged in, the system opens your **Dashboard**:

1. **Top Header Bar**:
    - **Active Shift Badge**: Shows which shift is currently operating (e.g., `Shift 1: 07:00–15:00 WIB`, `Shift 2: 15:00–23:00 WIB`, or `Shift 3: 23:00–07:00 WIB`).
    - **Live Clock**: Displays real-time factory time synchronized to Western Indonesian Time (`WIB`).
    - **Identity Pill**: Displays your NPK and color-coded role badge.
2. **Assignment Overview**:
    - Displays your assigned **Department** (e.g., _Stamping Department_) and **Section** (e.g., _Line A Stamping_).
3. **Role Capabilities**:
    - Summarizes the specific actions your account is permitted to carry out in the plant.

### Role Badge Reference

Your role determines what actions you can take in the system:

| Role                | Badge Color  | Primary Responsibilities                                              |
| ------------------- | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Administrator**   | Purple       | Plant-wide user setup, system settings, and audit logs                |
| **Manager**         | Blue         | Approving overtime timesheets and reviewing departmental budget burn  |
| **Team Leader**     | Green        | Submitting daily shift overtime and attaching physical SPKL sign-offs |
| **Operator / User** | Neutral Gray | Viewing personal overtime logs and confirming verified hours          |

---

### Signing Out Safely

To ensure work security and prevent unauthorized access at shift end:

1. Click your name or avatar in the bottom-left of the sidebar (or top-right on mobile).
2. Click the red **Sign Out** option.
3. An inline safety warning appears:
   _"Sign out of your account? Please ensure your current shift tasks are saved before leaving."_
4. Click **Yes, Sign Out** to safely close your session.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I log in using my NPK if I forgot my corporate email?**  
A: Yes. The login box accepts either your NPK badge number or your corporate email address with the same password.

**Q: What happens if I type the wrong password multiple times?**  
A: For plant security, after 5 incorrect password attempts, your account will be temporarily locked for 60 seconds. A warning banner with a live countdown timer will display on the screen. Once the timer reaches zero, you can try entering your password again.

**Q: Why do I see an "Access Restricted" screen when opening a link?**  
A: If a team leader or manager sends you a link to a section or departmental module that is outside your assigned unit, the system protects confidential data by showing a friendly "Access Restricted" screen. Simply click the **Return to Dashboard** button to return to your workspace.

**Q: What should I do if my account has been deactivated?**  
A: Contact the plant HR or IT helpdesk (Extension: 1204 or WhatsApp: +62 857-1583-8733) to verify your employment status and reactivate your profile.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue                                                 | Solution                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _"The provided credentials do not match our records"_ | Verify that your NPK or email is spelled correctly. Use the eye icon to verify that Caps Lock is not accidentally turned on.                                                     |
| Account locked countdown banner                       | Wait until the countdown reaches 0 seconds before attempting another login. If you have forgotten your password, contact your supervisor or IT Admin.                            |
| Department or Section displays _"Not Assigned"_       | New accounts may not yet have an assigned cost center. Contact your plant administrator or HR supervisor to update your department and section assignment in the master records. |
| Screen displays technical errors                      | Refresh the browser page. If the issue persists, contact IT Admin (+62 857-1583-8733).                                                                                           |

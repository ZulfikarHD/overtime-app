# Early Warning Alert Detail - User Guide

## What is the Alert Detail?

When a section uses too much of its daily overtime budget, the **Early Warning** menu lists it as an alert. The **alert detail** page explains one alert: how high the index was, how many days in a row it has been in alert, how that compares with the limits of the department, what happened over the last working days, and whether a follow-up already exists.

## How to Use

### Open an alert

1. Open the **Early Warning** menu in the sidebar
2. Click a row, or click **Follow Up**

### Read the page from top to bottom

1. **Header** — the section, the date, the department, the zone (**Kuning** or **Merah**), the index in percent and the streak, for example "3 consecutive days"
2. **Index bar** — the filled bar is the index of that day. The thin marks show where **Hijau** ends and **Kuning** ends for your department, and **Target 100%** is the plan itself
3. **Production volume** and **Manpower change** — show whether production or the number of people changed around the alert, compared with the previous month (see the [Alert Vitals guide](./early-warning-alert-vitals.md)). When the data is not available the card shows **N/A**; hover over the information icon to see why
4. **Last 7 working days** — a chart of the daily index with the green, yellow and red areas behind it. The large dot is the alert day
5. **Follow-up** — shows the status (**Not followed up yet**, **Draft** or **Terkirim**). A sent follow-up also shows when it was sent and by whom, and cannot be changed

### Read earlier follow-ups

1. When an alert has more than one follow-up, small buttons appear next to **Follow-up history**
2. Click a button to read that follow-up: what, why, when, where, who, how, and how much (cost in Rupiah and overtime hours)

### Go back to the list

1. Click **Back to Early Warning**
2. The list opens with the same filters you used before (department, dates, zone and status)

> 💡 **Tip:** You can copy the page address of an alert to share it with a colleague in the same department.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Why does the page say "No alert on this date"?**
A: On that day the section was in the **Hijau** zone, had no overtime budget, or the date has not happened yet. You can still read the chart, but no follow-up can be created for it.

**Q: Why do Production volume and Manpower change show N/A?**
A: The monthly data for the alert month or the month before has not been entered yet, or the earlier figure was zero. The page never shows an estimated number in its place.

**Q: Why can I not write a follow-up yet?**
A: Writing, saving and sending the 5W2H follow-up from this page arrives in a later release. Until then, this page shows the follow-ups that already exist.

**Q: Why does the chart not show 7 days?**
A: Only working days count, and a section without a budget for a month has no index for that month.

**Q: Why do I get an "Access Restricted" page?**
A: The section belongs to another department. Managers and Team Leaders only see their own department.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue                                        | Solution                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The page returns to the list with a red note | The date in the address is not a real date. Open the alert again from the **Early Warning** list            |
| The chart says there is no data              | The section has no overtime budget for that month, or no day has been recorded yet                          |
| **Back to Early Warning** shows all alerts   | You have not opened the list in this session, so there are no filters to remember. Choose the filters again |
| Access Restricted page                       | You do not have the Administrator, Manager or Team Leader role, or the section is not in your department    |

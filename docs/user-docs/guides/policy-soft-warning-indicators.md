# Overtime Policy Soft Warning Indicators - User Guide

## What are Policy Soft Warning Indicators?

The **Overtime Policy Soft Warning Indicators** help shift supervisors (Team Leaders) and Department Managers keep track of employee overtime limits and avoid fatigue-related safety hazards.

When you enter overtime hours for a worker who is approaching or exceeding company thresholds, the system shows clear advisory colored badges right next to the worker's name. These badges are **informational only** (BR-06)—they will never stop or block you from submitting an overtime shift.

---

## Warning Badge Types

| :---------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **⚠️ Batas mingguan terlampaui (X/Y jam)**                        | The employee's total overtime hours for the current week have exceeded the allowed limit (standard: 20 hours/week).   | Check whether overtime can be shared with other qualified operators on the line. | **🔴 Beban Kerja Tinggi: X minggu berturut-turut melebihi batas** | The employee has had heavy overtime (> 20 hours/week) for 3 or more consecutive weeks. ## How to View and Work with Policy Warnings

### 1. While Entering Daily Overtime (Team Leader)

1. Open the **Overtime Entry** menu in the sidebar.
2. Select your section crew or click **Pilih Semua (Add All)**.
3. Type the hours worked in the respective categories (Production, TPM, CapEx, or Others).
4. As soon as you finish typing or click away from the hours field, the system automatically calculates the worker's cumulative weekly hours.
5. If a limit is exceeded, an advisory yellow or red badge appears under the worker's name. Hover or tap the badge to see the exact hours calculation.
6. Verify the hours and click **Kirim Pengajuan Lembur**. The submission will be saved immediately without blocking.

> 💡 **Tip:** Policy warnings are advisory! You do not need to fill out exception forms or seek emergency IT clearance to submit overtime when badges appear.

---

### 2. While Reviewing Submissions (Department Manager)

1. Open the **Overtime Entry** menu in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Riwayat Pengajuan** (Submission History) tab.
3. Locate the shift batch and click **Detail** to open the submission inspection window.
4. Review the crew line items. Workers who exceeded policy thresholds will show their advisory badge directly on their timesheet row.
5. Use this information to coordinate rotation schedules or review section workload distribution.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I still submit overtime if a red warning badge appears?**  
**A:** Yes. Under ISUZU operational rule BR-06, soft policy warnings are strictly advisory. They will never prevent you from submitting legitimate shift overtime.

**Q: When does the overtime week start and end?**  
**A:** The weekly calculation starts on Monday at 00:00 WIB and resets on Sunday at 23:59 WIB (Western Indonesia Time).

**Q: Does our department have custom overtime limits?**  
**A:** The standard factory limit is 20.0 hours per week and 3 consecutive weeks alert. If your Department Manager configured a custom threshold, the system automatically uses your department's specific rules.

# SPKL Pending Reminders & In-App Notifications - User Guide

## What is SPKL Pending Reminders?

When you submit daily overtime for your shift, the system records your crew's hours immediately so shift operations can proceed without delays. However, company policy requires submitting a signed physical overtime order document (_Surat Perintah Kerja Lembur_ or SPKL) within a 48-hour grace period for payroll audit compliance.

The **SPKL Pending Reminder** system alerts you directly in the top navigation bar when an SPKL document is due tomorrow or is overdue. You can easily view the reminder and attach your physical document or registration number in just a few clicks without leaving your current screen.

---

## How to Use

### 1. Checking Your Notifications

1. Look at the **Bell icon** in the top navigation bar (located right next to the Live WIB Clock).
2. If you have pending or overdue SPKL documents, a **red counter badge** shows the number of unread alerts (for example, `1` or `2`).
3. Click the **Bell icon** to open the notification list.
4. Each notification card shows:
    - **Submission Code** (e.g. `OT-20260906-ASSY1-001`)
    - **Section and Due Date**
    - **Overdue Status Badge** (e.g., `1 days overdue` in red or `Due Tomorrow` in amber)
    - **Action Buttons**: **Attach** and **Mark as read**

---

### 2. Attaching an SPKL Document Directly from a Notification (User Journey 5)

You do not need to navigate away from your work to clear a reminder:

1. Click the **Bell icon** in the top navigation bar.
2. On the target notification card, click the **Attach** button.
3. The **Attach SPKL Document** panel will slide in from the right side of the screen with your submission details already pre-filled.
4. Either:
    - Drag and drop your scanned SPKL document (PDF, JPEG, or PNG up to 3 MB), or
    - Enter the printed **Physical SPKL Document Number** (for example, `SPKL/PROD/2026/IX/089`).
5. Click **Upload & Save SPKL**.
6. The panel will close, a confirmation message will appear, the reminder is automatically marked as resolved, and the red badge count decreases.

---

### 3. Dismissing Notifications

- To dismiss a single reminder without attaching a document, click the **Checkmark icon** on the notification card.
- To clear all notifications at once, click **Mark All as Read** at the top right of the notification dropdown.

---

### 4. Managing Your Reminder Preferences

If you wish to turn off SPKL reminder notifications:

1. Click your profile avatar in the top right corner and open **Settings → Preferences**.
2. Locate the **Notification Channels & Alerts** section.
3. Toggle the **SPKL Physical Document Reminders** switch on or off.
4. Click **Save Preferences**.

> 💡 **Tip:** We recommend keeping SPKL reminders enabled to prevent audit compliance flags during monthly payroll closing.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do overdue SPKL reminders prevent me from submitting new daily overtime?**  
A: No. Overtime submissions are never blocked by pending SPKL documents (Business Rule BR-05). You can always continue submitting daily shifts for your section.

**Q: When are reminder notifications sent?**  
A: The system automatically scans for pending documents every morning at **08:00 WIB**. It sends an advance reminder when an SPKL is due tomorrow and an overdue alert when the deadline has passed.

**Q: Can other team leaders see my overdue reminders?**  
A: No. SPKL notifications are strictly confidential to the supervisor who submitted the overtime batch and plant administrators.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue                                                                    | Solution                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The red badge shows on the bell, but clicking it shows no notifications. | Click the browser refresh button to ensure your session data is synchronized, or check if another supervisor attached the document. |
| I uploaded an SPKL file, but the notification did not disappear.         | Click the checkmark icon on the notification card to manually mark it as read, or click **Mark All as Read**.                       |
| I am not receiving reminder notifications at 08:00 WIB.                  | Go to **Settings → Preferences** and verify that the **SPKL Physical Document Reminders** option is turned on.                      |
